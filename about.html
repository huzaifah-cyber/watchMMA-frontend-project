About WatchMMA

Our Origin

WatchMMA started operations in 2019. It is the first fighting site in history to have an archive of all old fights. It also does not display ads (thanks to our supporters). We also stream all the latest fights from our own infrastructure. We have a collection of fights from the UFC, PFL, Boxer, and GFL.
